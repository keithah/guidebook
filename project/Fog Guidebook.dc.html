How to ride
Bay Area transit looks like alphabet soup from the outside. It isn't — here's the whole thing on one page.
Paying — pick one and forget it
Clipper in Apple or Google Pay
What I'd do. Add it once in Wallet, then hold your phone to the reader. Works on Muni, BART, Caltrain and every regional bus, and it gives you free transfers.
Contactless bank card
Tap your own card on Muni and BART. Simple, but no transfer credit.
Physical Clipper card
From the machines at Balboa Park BART if you'd rather not use a phone.
Kids ride free
Under 10 walks on with you on Muni — no tag, no fare. Youth up to 18 ride Muni free too, with a youth Clipper.
Muni — the city itself
Buses, trolleybuses, the Metro trains (J K L M N T) and the cable cars. One flat fare, ~$2.85, and it buys you 120 minutes of unlimited transfers if you tap with Clipper. Board at any door on the Metro; tap on entry on buses.

BART — the region
Regional trains to Oakland, Berkeley and SFO. Distance-based fare, so you tap in and tap out at the gates. Balboa Park is our station, 9 minutes on foot, and SFO is a straight ride.
CT
Caltrain — down the Peninsula
Electric trains to Palo Alto, Mountain View and San José from 4th & King. Tap in and out like BART. Take the K or T to get there. Worth it if you're heading to the South Bay for a day.
Leaving the city by bus
AC Transit — Oakland, Berkeley, the East Bay
Golden Gate Transit & Ferry — Marin, Sausalito, Larkspur
SamTrans — San Mateo County and the coast
All of them take the same Clipper in your Wallet.
Two habits that make this easy: check departures before you walk out (Google Maps isn't reliable for Muni — use the Transit app or 511.org), and always tap off on BART and Caltrain.

SF tips & safety
Fog and wind are real — carry a layer even in July. Never leave anything visible in a parked car, not a jacket, not a charger. Tip 18–20% at restaurants. If the ground shakes: drop, cover, hold on.
