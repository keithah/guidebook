Section 4
The Cottage
600 square feet, two bedrooms, and a shared courtyard with a firepit. Here's how everything works — including the two things people always ask me about.
Living Room
Sectional converts to a bed — use the foam topper, it makes all the difference. 50" TV on a swing arm.
Bedroom 2 · loft
Queen bed, lots of closet. Low ceiling at the sides — watch your head getting up.
Kitchen
Keurig for one cup, the 12-cup drip for a crowd. Sodastream under the counter.
Laundry
Free stackable washer/dryer, behind the kitchen door.
Trash day is Tuesday
Three bins: blue recycling, green compost, black landfill. SF is serious about this one — compost really does mean food scraps.
